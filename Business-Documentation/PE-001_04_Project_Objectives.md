# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the business and technical objectives of the Business Intelligence modernization initiative for Adventure Works Cycles.

These objectives establish the direction of the project and provide measurable outcomes against which success can be evaluated.

---

# Project Vision

To transform Adventure Works Cycles into a more data-driven organization by modernizing its analytical platform and enabling faster, more informed business decision-making through Business Intelligence and cloud technologies.

---

# Business Objectives

The project aims to achieve the following business objectives:

## 1. Improve Decision-Making

Provide management with timely, accurate, and consistent business information that supports operational, tactical, and strategic decisions.

---

## 2. Standardize Business Reporting

Establish a consistent reporting framework with standardized KPI definitions across departments.

---

## 3. Increase Business Visibility

Enable executives to monitor sales performance, customer trends, product profitability, and regional performance through centralized dashboards.

---

## 4. Reduce Manual Reporting

Minimize the time and effort required to prepare recurring management reports by introducing automated reporting processes.

> **Assumption:** Existing management reports require manual preparation before distribution.

---

## 5. Support Future Growth

Design an analytical solution capable of supporting increasing business volumes and future reporting requirements.

---

# Technical Objectives

The project will also demonstrate the following technical objectives:

- Design a structured SQL Server analytical environment.
- Optimize SQL queries for reporting workloads.
- Demonstrate migration to Azure SQL Database.
- Develop interactive Power BI dashboards.
- Create reusable business measures and KPIs.
- Document the complete analytical solution.

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it demonstrates the ability to:

- Deliver standardized executive reporting.
- Improve accessibility of business information.
- Reduce dependence on manual reporting activities.
- Present consistent KPI calculations.
- Demonstrate cloud readiness through Azure SQL Database.
- Deliver interactive Business Intelligence dashboards.
- Provide actionable business recommendations supported by data.

# Project Scope

The project includes:

- Business analysis
- Data assessment
- SQL Server optimization
- Azure SQL migration demonstration
- Power BI dashboard development
- Business Intelligence reporting
- Consulting recommendations

The project does not include:

- Production deployment
- Live operational monitoring
- Organizational change management
- End-user training
- Enterprise security implementation

These exclusions are appropriate for a portfolio demonstration project.

---

# Expected Business Benefits

Successful completion of the project is expected to demonstrate how organizations can:

- Improve reporting efficiency.
- Increase confidence in business data.
- Accelerate executive decision-making.
- Support digital transformation initiatives.
- Establish a scalable analytical foundation for future growth.

---

# Consultant's Perspective

Technology alone does not create business value.

The value of this project lies in aligning data, analytics, and cloud technologies with clearly defined business objectives that support better decision-making.

Every technical implementation within this engagement is intended to contribute directly to a measurable business outcome.
