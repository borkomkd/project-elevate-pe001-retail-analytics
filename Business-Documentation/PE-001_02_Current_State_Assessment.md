# Purpose

The purpose of this assessment is to understand the current analytical environment at Adventure Works Cycles and identify the business and technical challenges that limit effective decision-making.

The findings in this document establish the baseline for designing a modern Business Intelligence solution.

---

# Current Business Environment

Adventure Works Cycles operates across multiple sales territories, product categories, and customer segments. Daily business operations generate significant volumes of transactional data related to sales, products, customers, inventory, and purchasing.

Although this data is available, management experiences increasing difficulty transforming operational data into timely and actionable business insights.

---

# Existing Reporting Process

The current reporting process is assumed to include:

- Periodic operational reports generated from SQL Server.
- Spreadsheet-based analysis for ad hoc reporting.
- Department-specific reports with limited standardization.
- Manual preparation of management reports.
- Limited executive dashboard capabilities.

As a result, business users spend considerable time collecting and validating information before meaningful analysis can begin.

---

# Current Data Landscape

The organization maintains operational data across several functional areas, including:

- Sales
- Customers
- Products
- Orders
- Inventory
- Purchasing
- Employees
- Sales Territories

These datasets provide valuable business information but are primarily optimized for daily operations rather than analytical reporting.

---

# Identified Business Pain Points

The following challenges have been identified during the assessment:

- Reporting processes require significant manual effort.
- Business users rely on multiple report versions.
- KPI definitions are not fully standardized.
- Historical trend analysis requires additional manual preparation.
- Executive reporting is not always available in real time.
- Growing data volumes create performance challenges for complex queries.

---

# Technology Assessment

Current analytical capabilities include:

- Microsoft SQL Server as the operational database.
- Traditional SQL queries for reporting.
- Limited visualization capabilities.
- Minimal cloud integration.
- No centralized Business Intelligence platform.

---

# Opportunities for Improvement

Several opportunities have been identified:

- Standardize KPI definitions across departments.
- Centralize analytical reporting.
- Improve SQL query performance.
- Introduce interactive Power BI dashboards.
- Migrate analytical workloads to Azure SQL Database.
- Reduce manual reporting activities.
- Establish a scalable cloud-ready analytics platform.

---

# Assessment Summary

Adventure Works possesses valuable business data but lacks a modern analytical platform that supports efficient reporting and executive decision-making.

The assessment indicates that the organization would benefit from a centralized Business Intelligence solution combining SQL Server optimization, Azure SQL Database, and Power BI to improve reporting efficiency, scalability, and business insight.
