# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the primary business challenges facing Adventure Works Cycles and explain why the organization has initiated a Business Intelligence modernization project.

The identified challenges will serve as the foundation for the proposed analytical solution and guide the project's objectives and recommendations.

---

# Business Challenge Overview

Adventure Works Cycles has experienced continued business growth across products, customers, and sales territories. As operations expand, management requires increasingly accurate, timely, and consistent information to support strategic and operational decision-making.

While the organization possesses valuable business data, the existing reporting environment does not fully support the speed, scalability, and analytical capabilities expected from a modern data-driven organization.

The company has therefore decided to evaluate opportunities to modernize its reporting platform using Microsoft Business Intelligence technologies.

---

# Key Business Challenges

## 1. Delayed Decision-Making

Management often waits for reports to be prepared before evaluating business performance.

**Business Impact**

- Slower reaction to market changes
- Delayed operational decisions
- Reduced management agility

---

## 2. Inconsistent Reporting

Different departments may interpret or calculate business metrics differently.

**Business Impact**

- Inconsistent KPI reporting
- Reduced confidence in reported figures
- Additional effort validating information

> **Assumption:** Some business metrics are calculated independently by different departments before being consolidated into management reports.

---

## 3. Limited Business Visibility

Executives require a consolidated view of sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and regional trends.

Current reporting provides only partial visibility into these areas.

**Business Impact**

- Difficult prioritization of improvement initiatives
- Limited ability to identify growth opportunities
- Reactive rather than proactive decision-making

---

## 4. Increasing Data Volumes

As business transactions continue to grow, analytical queries become more demanding.

**Business Impact**

- Longer report execution times
- Reduced reporting efficiency
- Increased pressure on operational systems

---

## 5. Need for Modern Analytics

The organization recognizes that traditional reporting methods no longer satisfy evolving business requirements.

Management seeks a modern Business Intelligence platform capable of providing interactive dashboards, standardized KPIs, and scalable cloud-based analytics.

---

# Why Modernization Is Being Considered

The organization is evaluating modernization to achieve the following outcomes:

- Faster access to business insights
- Improved reporting consistency
- Standardized KPI definitions
- Better executive decision support
- Reduced manual reporting effort
- Scalable analytical infrastructure
- Readiness for future digital transformation initiatives

---

# Risks of Maintaining the Current State

If no modernization effort is undertaken, the organization may experience:

- Continued reporting inefficiencies
- Increasing maintenance effort
- Slower business response times
- Reduced competitiveness
- Growing complexity in reporting processes
- Difficulty scaling analytical capabilities

---

# Opportunity Statement

This project presents an opportunity to transform operational data into a centralized Business Intelligence platform that supports informed decision-making, improves reporting efficiency, and establishes a foundation for future cloud adoption and digital transformation.

---

# Consultant's Initial Perspective

Based on the current assessment, the primary challenge is not a lack of data.

The organization already possesses valuable operational information.

The challenge lies in transforming that information into consistent, timely, and actionable business intelligence that supports confident decision-making at all levels of the organization.
