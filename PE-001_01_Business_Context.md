# Executive Summary

Adventure Works Cycles is an international manufacturer and retailer of bicycles and cycling accessories. As the organization has grown, its business operations, reporting requirements, and data volumes have become increasingly complex.

Management relies on data from multiple operational systems to monitor sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and regional performance. Although large amounts of data are available, reporting processes are fragmented, time-consuming, and provide limited support for strategic decision-making.

This consulting engagement demonstrates how a modern Business Intelligence platform can improve decision-making by centralizing data, optimizing SQL Server workloads, migrating analytical databases to Azure SQL Database, and delivering interactive Power BI dashboards.

The project follows a structured consulting approach, focusing not only on technical implementation but also on delivering measurable business value.

---

# Client Overview

Adventure Works Cycles is a multinational company specializing in the manufacturing and distribution of bicycles, cycling equipment, and accessories.

The company operates through multiple sales channels, serves customers across different geographic regions, and manages a diverse product portfolio.

To remain competitive, management requires timely, accurate, and consistent information to support operational and strategic decisions.

---

# Industry Context

The retail and manufacturing industries increasingly rely on data-driven decision-making.

Organizations that modernize their analytical platforms benefit from:

- Faster reporting
- Improved operational visibility
- Better inventory management
- Enhanced customer insights
- Scalable cloud infrastructure
- Improved executive decision support

Companies that continue relying on fragmented reporting often experience slower decision-making and inconsistent business metrics.

# Business Model

Adventure Works generates revenue through the sale of:

- Bicycles
- Bicycle Components
- Clothing
- Accessories

Sales are conducted through multiple sales territories using distributors, resellers, and direct customer channels.

Success depends on understanding:
